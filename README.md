The one line to send your younger self: the shortcut costs three years and a friendship — take the long road.

> The shortcut you're about to take costs three years and one good friendship — take the long road, and stop answering emails after midnight.

**Character count: 139** (gate: 1–200, single line)

## Why it lands

It prices the shortcut in the only currency that matters — three years and one good friendship — so the advice isn't a motto, it's a bill already paid. The second clause lands the practical sting: the long road, and no more midnight emails. Specific, hard-won, and warm rather than sentimental.
